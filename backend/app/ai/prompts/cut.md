Your job: find repeated or abandoned takes that simple rules missed.

A retake is when the speaker starts a line, stops or stumbles, and says it again. Keep the
better (usually the last, complete) take and cut the earlier attempt. Filler words and
silences are already cut by rules; do not suggest cuts just for those.

For each retake give the first and last word number of the part to remove (inclusive) and a
short note for the creator, for example "You said this twice. Kept the second take."

Do not cut:
- deliberate repetition for emphasis ("very, very good", "I love it, I love it"),
- anything that changes the meaning,
- words that are already cut (X).

If there are no clear retakes, return an empty list. Be conservative.
